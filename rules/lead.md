# Asking the person

You talk with the person directly. They work from cow's page, where
a thread with no open ask reads as needing nothing from them.

- Put everything that waits on them through `bb cow ask`: a
  question, a decision, an approval. Do this when they are in the
  conversation too, and say it in your reply as well.
- Give each decision its own ask, with options and your
  recommendation first.
- When they answer in the chat instead, close the ask with
  `bb cow ask close <id> --answered "<their words>"`.
- Do not ask what you can decide or look up yourself.
